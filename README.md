## Project Overview

Build & Deploy a Real AI-Powered Car Rental, Repair & Sales SaaS 🚗 | Next.js, AI Voice Agents & Admin Dashboard

Build & Deploy Drivelink — a Real-World AI-Powered Automobile Business SaaS!

In this video, we're showcasing Drivelink, a real-world SaaS platform built for an automobile business offering car rentals, car repairs, and car buying and selling.

This isn't just a demo project. It's a complete business platform with a customer website, customer portal, admin dashboard, and AI voice agents designed to handle real customer inquiries and bookings.

### 🚗 What We're Building:

## 1. Car Rental Management

- Search cars by availability and rental dates.
- Get instant quotes and book vehicles.
- Support self-drive and chauffeur-driven rentals.
- Offer vehicle pickup, delivery, deposits, and full payments.
- Allow customers to list their own cars for rent.

## 2. Car Repair & Service Booking

- Browse repair services and book appointments.
- Add vehicle details and select time slots.
- Support online and in-store payments.

## 3. Car Sales Marketplace

- Browse cars for sale.
- Schedule test drives and viewings.
- Reserve vehicles with refundable deposits.
- Support online purchases and customer inquiries.

## 4. AI Voice Agents

- Dedicated AI agents for rentals, repairs, and sales.
- An AI front-desk receptionist.
- Live access to vehicle availability, prices, policies, and FAQs.
- Bookings directly through voice calls.
- Automatic lead creation for callbacks when eligible calls disconnect.

## 5. Customer Portal & Admin Dashboard

- Manage bookings, purchases, payments, invoices, and messages.
- Manage inventory and bulk-import cars using CSV.
- Control individual services and features dynamically.
- Collect customer sign-ups for services marked as coming soon.

🎯 The Goal: Build and deploy a real-world SaaS application that combines full-stack development, AI voice automation, and practical business management into one platform.
